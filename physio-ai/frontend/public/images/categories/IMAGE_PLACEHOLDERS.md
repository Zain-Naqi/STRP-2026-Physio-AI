# Exercise Category Image Placeholder Specification & Photography Guidelines

This document outlines the recommended image specs, file names, folder paths, dimensions, and visual aesthetic guidelines for photography placeholders across all 11 exercise categories in **PhysioAI**.

---

## Visual Direction & Photography Aesthetic

* **Style**: Minimalist, soft natural light, warm organic color grading, close-up human focus.
* **Tone**: Calm, therapeutic, dignified, encouraging, tactile.
* **Avoid**: High-contrast gym neon lighting, generic stock plastic models, overly dramatic fitness sweat, cyber/futuristic overlays, robotic graphics.
* **Recommended Dimensions**: `1200 x 900 px` (4:3 aspect ratio), minimum `800 x 600 px`.
* **File Format**: WebP or compressed JPEG (< 180 KB).
